**Short answer:** For a first cologne, choose something easy to like, affordable and versatile. Popular picks include Versace Eros (sweet and fun), Carolina Herrera 212 Men (clean, office- and school-friendly), Azzaro Wanted (fresh-spicy all-rounder) and, on a budget, Afnan 9 PM.

## What to look for

- **Versatility** over uniqueness for your first bottle.
- **Moderate strength** for school, work and close quarters.
- **A price you won't regret** if your taste changes.
