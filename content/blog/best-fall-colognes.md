**Short answer:** Fall suits scents with spice, warmth and woods: cardamom, pepper, tonka, vanilla, cedar. Versace Eros Flame, Azzaro Wanted and Le Male Le Parfum are easy picks that bridge late summer into cooler evenings.

Fall is when fragrance really comes alive. Cooler air lets warmer, spicier scents bloom without feeling heavy, and you can start reaching for the bottles that felt like too much in July.

## What works in fall

- Spice: cardamom, cinnamon, black pepper.
- Warmth: tonka, vanilla, amber.
- Woods and earth: cedar, vetiver, patchouli.

Warm scents project more as the day heats up, so start with fewer sprays than you'd use with a fresh summer scent.
