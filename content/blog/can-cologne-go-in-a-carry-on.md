**Short answer:** Yes. Under TSA's liquids rule, cologne in containers of 3.4 oz (100 ml) or less can go in your carry-on, inside your one quart-size liquids bag. Larger bottles need to go in checked luggage. Always check the latest TSA guidance before you fly.

## Carry-on tips

- **Check the size on the bottle.** It's the container size that counts, not how full it is.
- **Use a travel atomizer** to decant a few sprays from a big bottle.
- **Pack it in your liquids bag** so screening is quick.

## Checked bags

Full-size bottles can go in checked luggage. Wrap them in clothing and keep the cap on to protect the sprayer.
