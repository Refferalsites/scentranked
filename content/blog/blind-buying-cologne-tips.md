Buying a fragrance you've never smelled is called a "blind buy". It's common when shopping online, and with a bit of homework it doesn't have to be a gamble.

## 1. Read the notes, not the adjectives

"Seductive" and "bold" tell you nothing. Notes like *bergamot, lavender, vanilla, cedar* tell you a lot. Learn the basics in our [fragrance families guide](/blog/fragrance-families-explained/).

## 2. Start with famous, well-loved scents

Fragrances that have stayed popular for years usually have broad appeal. They're the safest blind buys.

## 3. Match the season and setting

Buying in October? A warm, spicy scent will get more wear right away than an aquatic one.

## 4. Compare to what you already like

If you love a fragrance you own, look for others in the same family.

## 5. Check the size and concentration

Make sure you're comparing the same size and the same EDT vs EDP when looking at prices.

## 6. Consider value

An affordable bottle is a much lower-risk experiment than a niche luxury one.
