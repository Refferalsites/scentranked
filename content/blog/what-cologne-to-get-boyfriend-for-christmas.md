**Short answer:** Match the style he already wears. If he likes clean and fresh, Bleu de Chanel is a safe, versatile choice. If he likes sweet and warm, winter favorites like Azzaro The Most Wanted or Le Male Le Parfum are popular picks. When in doubt, choose a famous crowd-pleaser.

## Step 1: Peek at his current bottle

Whatever he wears now tells you whether he leans fresh, sweet, spicy or woody.

## Step 2: Think about his days

Office or school most days? Go fresh and moderate. Lots of nights out? Go warmer and sweeter.
