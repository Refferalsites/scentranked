Cologne is a classic gift, but choosing for someone else is tricky. The trick is to play it safe: widely loved scents, the right style for their life, and a full retail box rather than a tester.

## How to choose

1. **Notice what he wears now.** Fresh and clean, or warm and sweet? Stay in that lane.
2. **Think about his days.** Office-heavy? Choose something fresh and versatile.
3. **Go famous.** Well-known classics are popular for a reason.
4. **Check the packaging.** For gifts, pick the boxed retail version, not a tester.
