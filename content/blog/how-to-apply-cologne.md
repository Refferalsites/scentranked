**Short answer:** Spray 2–4 times onto clean, moisturized skin on your chest or the base of your neck, holding the bottle a few inches away. Use fewer sprays for strong, sweet scents, and don't rub your wrists together.

Most cologne mistakes come down to two things: spraying too much, or spraying in the wrong place. Here's a simple routine that works for nearly any fragrance.

## Where to spray

- **Chest or base of the neck:** body heat lifts the scent gently, and it sits under your shirt.
- **Sides of the neck:** classic, but go lighter here, since this spot is right at nose level for others.
- **Wrists:** fine, but **don't rub them together**. Rubbing doesn't "bruise" the scent, but it does speed up the evaporation of the bright top notes.

## How many sprays?

There's no universal number, because some fragrances are much stronger than others. A sensible starting point:

1. **Fresh / daytime scents:** 3 to 4 sprays.
2. **Sweet or intense evening scents:** 1 to 2 sprays.
3. **Office or close quarters:** err on the low side. If people can smell you from across the room, it's too much.

Loud, sweet fragrances like Versace Eros are the easiest to overdo. Two sprays is plenty for most settings.

## Nose blindness is real

After a few minutes your brain tunes out your own scent. That doesn't mean it's gone. Resist the urge to respray just because *you* can't smell it. Ask a friend instead.

## Make it last longer

- Apply right after a shower, onto clean, moisturized skin.
- An unscented moisturizer underneath can help.
- A light spray on clothing lasts longer than on skin (check delicate fabrics first).

## Pick the right scent for the weather

Heat amplifies sweet scents and makes them cloying. In summer, reach for something fresher.
