**Short answer:** Mostly, yes. Le Male's creamy vanilla-tonka base makes it a natural fit for fall and winter, while its fresh mint-lavender opening keeps it wearable on mild spring days. For deep winter, Le Male Le Parfum is warmer and richer.

## Why Le Male suits the cold

Le Male pairs a barbershop opening (mint, lavender, cardamom) with a sweet vanilla, tonka and amber base. Cold weather keeps that sweetness smooth instead of cloying.

## EDT vs Le Parfum

- **Le Male EDT:** lighter, fresher, more casual.
- **Le Male Le Parfum:** less mint, more cardamom, iris and vanilla. Dressier and denser.
