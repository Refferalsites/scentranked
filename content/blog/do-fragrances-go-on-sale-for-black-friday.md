**Short answer:** Often, yes. Many retailers discount fragrance around Black Friday and Cyber Monday (Black Friday is November 27, 2026). But deals vary by store, brand and size, so compare the current price with what it usually costs before you buy.

## How to spot a real deal

1. **Note the regular price** now, before the sales start.
2. **Compare the same size and concentration.**
3. **Check shipping** and delivery dates if it's a gift.
