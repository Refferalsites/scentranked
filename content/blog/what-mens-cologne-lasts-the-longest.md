**Short answer:** Longevity depends on concentration and notes, as well as your skin and climate. Eau de parfum and parfum versions with warm bases (vanilla, amber, tonka, woods, oud) usually outlast fresh citrus scents. Le Male Le Parfum, 1 Million Parfum and Creed Royal Oud are good examples.

## What makes a fragrance last

- **Higher concentration:** EDP and parfum have more perfume oil.
- **Heavier base notes:** vanilla, amber, resins and woods evaporate slowly.
- **Your skin:** moisturized skin holds scent better.
