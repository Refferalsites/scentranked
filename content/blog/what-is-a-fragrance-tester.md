**Short answer:** A tester is a bottle made for stores to let shoppers sample a fragrance. It typically holds the same fragrance as the retail version but comes in plainer packaging, often without a box or cap, and usually costs less. It's a good buy for yourself, less so as a gift.

Shopping for cologne online, you'll sometimes see the word **tester** in a listing. Here's what that usually means.

## What a tester is

Testers are bottles made for stores to let shoppers sample a fragrance. They typically contain the same fragrance as the retail version, but the packaging is simpler: often a plain box (or no box), and sometimes no cap.

## Pros

- Usually cheaper than the boxed retail bottle.
- Same juice, if it's a genuine tester of the same concentration.

## Cons

- Plainer presentation, which matters if it's a gift.
- Packaging and cap may differ from the retail version.

## When it makes sense

If the fragrance is for you, not a present, a tester can be a sensible way to get a full-size bottle for less. Always check the listing for concentration (EDT vs EDP) and size so you're comparing like for like.
