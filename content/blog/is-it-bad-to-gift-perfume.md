**Short answer:** No, fragrance is a popular, thoughtful gift. The only risk is choosing a scent the person doesn't like. Reduce it by matching their current style, choosing a widely loved classic, or picking a smaller bottle if you're unsure.

## Why it can feel risky

Scent is personal, and some people are attached to their signature fragrance.

## How to make it land

1. **Notice what they wear now.**
2. **Pick a famous, versatile scent.**
3. **Keep the receipt and packaging** in case of exchange.
