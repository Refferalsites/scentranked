**Short answer:** Start with where you'll wear it most, pick a scent style you already like (fresh, aromatic, warm or woody), test it on skin and judge it after an hour, and make your first bottle a versatile one. Add a contrasting scent later.

A "signature scent" is just the fragrance people come to associate with you. You don't need an expensive one. You need one that suits your life and that you enjoy wearing day after day.

## Step 1: Start with where you'll wear it

Be honest about your week. If most of your hours are at an office or around people up close, you want something clean and moderate. If you mainly wear fragrance for nights out, you can go warmer and bolder.

## Step 2: Pick a direction, not a bottle

Most men's colognes fall into a few broad styles:

- **Fresh:** citrus, marine, green. Easy, clean, daytime-friendly.
- **Aromatic:** lavender, herbs, mint. Classic "barbershop" feel.
- **Warm/sweet:** vanilla, tonka, amber, spice. Cozy, sensual, great for evenings.
- **Woody:** cedar, sandalwood, vetiver. Grounded and grown-up.

Notice which of these you already enjoy in candles, soaps or other people's cologne. That's a strong clue.

## Step 3: Test on skin, then wait

The first spray is the loud top notes. The scent you actually live with appears 30 to 60 minutes later. Test one fragrance per wrist at most, and judge it a few hours in.

## Step 4: Choose a versatile first pick

If you're unsure, start with something that covers lots of situations. A fresh-woody classic is a common choice because it works at work, on weekends and at dinner.

Once you have an everyday scent, a second bottle in the opposite style (say, something warm for evenings) covers almost everything else.
