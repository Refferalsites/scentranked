**Short answer:** The usual causes are dry skin, a light concentration (eau de toilette or cologne), fresh citrus notes that evaporate quickly, and nose blindness, where you stop smelling your own scent even though others can. Moisturize first, spray a little on clothing, or choose an eau de parfum.

## 1. Dry skin

Fragrance clings better to moisturized skin. Apply an unscented lotion right after a shower, then spray.

## 2. Light concentration

Eau de toilette and eau de cologne contain less perfume oil than eau de parfum or parfum.

## 3. Fresh notes fade first

Citrus and aquatic notes are naturally volatile. Warm notes like vanilla, amber and woods last longer.

## 4. Nose blindness

After a while your brain tunes your own scent out. Ask a friend before you respray.
