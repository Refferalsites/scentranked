Cold air makes fragrances feel quieter and slower to evaporate. That's why winter is the season for denser, warmer and sweeter scents. They have the body to come through layers of clothing.

## What to look for

- Higher concentrations (EDP, Parfum) that hold up longer.
- Warm bases: amber, vanilla, tonka, resins.
- Spice and leather for richness.

## Wearing tip

Spray on skin under your clothes (chest, neck) instead of on your coat. Body heat helps the scent rise, and coats get cleaned less often.
