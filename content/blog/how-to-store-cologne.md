A good bottle of cologne can last a long time, but only if you store it well. Fragrance breaks down because of three things: **heat, light and air**. Keep those under control and your bottles will stay true much longer.

## Don't keep it in the bathroom

It's the most common spot, and one of the worst. Showers create repeated swings in heat and humidity. A bedroom drawer, a closet shelf, or the original box are all better choices.

## Keep it out of sunlight

UV light can change both the color and the smell of a fragrance. That pretty bottle on the windowsill is aging faster than it would in a drawer.

## Keep the cap on

Less air exposure means slower oxidation. Spray bottles are already fairly well sealed, but replacing the cap keeps dust out and protects the nozzle.

## Avoid the car

Cars get extremely hot in summer, especially across much of the US. Carry a small travel atomizer if you want to top up during the day, and leave your main bottle at home.

## Signs a fragrance has turned

- The opening smells sour, sharp or "off" compared with how you remember it.
- The liquid has darkened noticeably. Some darkening can be natural, so trust your nose more than your eyes.
- Bright citrus top notes seem to have disappeared.
