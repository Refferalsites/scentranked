**Short answer:** Taste is personal, so there's no universal answer. Clean, fresh scents (like Bleu de Chanel) and warm, smooth vanilla-amber scents (like Le Male Le Parfum) are the styles most often recommended as widely liked. Wearing it lightly matters as much as the bottle.

## Styles that tend to go over well

- **Clean and fresh:** citrus, light woods, soft aromatics.
- **Warm and smooth:** vanilla, tonka, amber, gentle spice.
