**Short answer:** Yes, Eros Flame is at its best in fall and winter. It opens with zesty citrus and black pepper, then settles into warm vanilla, tonka and woods, which works beautifully on cool days and evenings. In summer, keep it to nights and use a light hand.

## Eros vs Eros Flame

- **Eros (original):** cold mint and green apple over sweet vanilla. Louder and more youthful.
- **Eros Flame:** citrus and pepper over warm woods and vanilla. Warmer and more grown-up.
