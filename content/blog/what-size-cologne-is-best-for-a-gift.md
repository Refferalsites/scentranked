**Short answer:** If you know he loves the scent, a standard full-size bottle (often 3.4 oz / 100 ml) is great value. If you're not sure, a smaller bottle (around 1.6–2.5 oz) is a lower-risk gift. A travel spray makes a nice stocking stuffer.

## Know his taste? Go big

Bigger bottles usually cost less per ml, and a favorite scent gets used up.

## Not sure? Go smaller

A smaller bottle lets him try something new without a big commitment.
