**Short answer:** The best gift set is one built around a fragrance he'll actually wear. Pick the scent first (a versatile classic is safest) and treat the extras (travel spray, shower gel, aftershave balm) as a bonus. If no set exists for the scent you want, a single full-size bottle is just as good a gift.

## What's usually in a set

Gift sets often pair a full or medium bottle with a travel-size spray and matching body products. Contents vary by brand and season, so check the listing.

## How to choose

1. **Choose the scent, not the box.**
2. **Check the bottle size** in the set.
3. **Favor famous crowd-pleasers** if you're unsure of his taste.
