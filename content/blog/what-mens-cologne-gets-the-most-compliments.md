**Short answer:** No study ranks colognes by compliments, but the same names come up again and again in fragrance communities: Versace Eros, Bleu de Chanel, Jean Paul Gaultier Le Male Le Parfum and Azzaro The Most Wanted. They share good projection and a likeable sweet or fresh profile.

## Why some scents get noticed

Compliments usually come from three things: people can smell it (projection), it's pleasant rather than challenging, and it feels familiar enough to read as "attractive". Sweet vanilla-amber and clean fresh-woody scents tick those boxes.

## How to actually get compliments

- **Don't overspray.** Two sprays of a strong scent beats six.
- **Match the setting.** Fresh in the office, sweet at night.
- **Wear what you like.** Confidence matters more than the bottle.
