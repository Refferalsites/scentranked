**Short answer:** Yes, lightly. Choose a fresh, moderate fragrance and wear one to three sprays so people notice it only when they're close to you. Check whether your workplace has a fragrance-free policy first.

A good work fragrance makes you seem put-together without announcing itself. The rule of thumb: people should notice it when they're close to you, not from across the room.

## What tends to work at the office

- **Fresh, clean profiles:** citrus, light woods, soft aromatics.
- **Moderate strength:** many Eau de Toilettes are ideal here.
- **Nothing very sweet or smoky:** save heavy vanilla, oud and loud gourmands for evenings.

## How much to wear

One to three sprays, applied at home, usually does it. Shared spaces like elevators, meetings and open-plan offices amplify fragrance. If you're unsure, go lighter. Some workplaces also have fragrance-free policies, so check first.

## A quick checklist

1. Can someone smell it at arm's length? Good.
2. Can they smell it from their desk? Too much.
3. Does it still smell pleasant after lunch? Then it's a keeper.
