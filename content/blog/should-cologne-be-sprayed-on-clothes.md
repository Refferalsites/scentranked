**Short answer:** You can. Fabric often holds scent longer than skin. But skin lets the fragrance warm up and develop, and some fragrances can mark delicate or light fabrics. Many people spray mostly on skin and add a light spray on clothing.

## Skin: the default

Body heat helps a fragrance unfold from its top notes to its base. The chest and neck are reliable spots.

## Clothes: longer lasting

Cotton and wool tend to hold scent for a long time. Spray from a distance and let it dry before you get dressed.

## Watch out for

- **Stains:** colored or oily fragrances can mark silk and light fabrics. Test on a hidden spot.
- **Overdoing it:** scent on clothing can linger into tomorrow.
