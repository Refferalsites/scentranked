**Short answer:** Versace Eros works best in cooler weather: fall, winter and spring evenings. Its sweet vanilla-tonka base can feel heavy and cloying in summer heat, though one or two sprays on a summer night is fine.

## Why Eros suits the cold

Eros opens with cool mint and green apple, then dries down into sweet vanilla, tonka and ambroxan. Cold air tames sweetness and lets it project nicely. Heat amplifies it.

## Wearing it in summer

- Use 1–2 sprays, at night only.
- Spray on the chest under your shirt, not the neck.
- Or switch to a fresh aquatic scent for hot days.
