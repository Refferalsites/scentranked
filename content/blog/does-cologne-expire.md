**Short answer:** Cologne doesn't have a strict expiration date, but it does slowly degrade. Kept away from heat, light and air, most bottles stay good for several years. If the opening smells sour, sharp or "off", or the bright top notes have vanished, it has turned.

## What breaks fragrance down

Heat, light and oxygen. Every time air gets in, and every time the bottle sits in sun or a steamy bathroom, the delicate top notes (especially citrus) degrade a little faster.

## Signs your cologne has gone bad

- The first spray smells sour, metallic or vinegary.
- Fresh citrus notes seem to have disappeared.
- The liquid has darkened a lot. Some color change can be natural, so trust your nose first.

## How to make it last

1. Store it in a cool, dark place, like a drawer or closet, not the bathroom.
2. Keep the cap on and the box if you have it.
3. Don't leave bottles in the car.
