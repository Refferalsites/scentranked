**Short answer:** Choose a scent in the style he already likes, buy a boxed retail bottle (not a tester), confirm the size and concentration, and order early enough for holiday shipping. A short note about why you picked it makes it personal.

## 1. Pick the style

Fresh for everyday, warm and sweet for evenings and winter.

## 2. Buy retail, not a tester

Testers are great value for yourself but come in plain packaging. See [What Is a Fragrance Tester?](/blog/what-is-a-fragrance-tester/).

## 3. Check size and concentration

Compare like for like (EDT vs EDP, oz or ml).

## 4. Order early

Shipping gets busy in December.
